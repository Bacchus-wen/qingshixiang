Close shot inside a dusty 1990s Chinese home-appliance repair shop at dusk: a small CRT television on a stack of cardboard boxes showing a warm 1990s TV programme, beside it an old tube radio whose dial window glows amber, on the steel shelf behind a green desk fan, a cassette boombox and an enamel thermos. A warm circular pool of light from a small robot's camera-lens eye rests on the TV and the radio: inside the pool everything looks as it did decades ago, clean and alive; outside it the objects are dusty, grey and cold. Shallow depth of field like an f/2 lens, the TV screen and the radio dial in sharp focus, soft bokeh on the shelf behind, a faint beam of light in the dusty air entering from the side.
