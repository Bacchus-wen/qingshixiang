Same view from inside the shop behind the small robot (head in profile in the lower right, its lens eye flickering weakly): the whole row of shophouses across the street is back in the 1990s evening for one last moment, every shop open and lit, laundry on the balconies, neighbours outside. The electronics shop with a television glowing in its window, which the robot is looking at, is the brightest and in the centre of attention; the other shops are progressively dimmer, a few windows still dark. Warm light from the shopfronts spills across the old cracked road towards the robot's feet.
