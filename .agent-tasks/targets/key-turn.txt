Seen from just outside a half-open steel rolling shutter at dusk, with a dark door frame in the near foreground: the small handmade robot (wooden radio-cabinet head with one camera-lens eye, dented sheet-metal chassis with a desk-fan motor, pipe arms and legs with brass tap joints, worn boots) has swivelled round on its low wooden stool to face the street, its lens glowing warm white. Head to feet, the stool and its shadow fully in frame. Cool blue dusk light from the street models its front and rims its edges; the warm desk lamp behind it stays secondary; the dim repair shop full of old appliances behind.
